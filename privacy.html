Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026. Collector Advantage is in development, and this policy will be updated before it opens to the public.

This policy explains what Collector Advantage ("we", "us") collects when you use our website, web app and browser extension, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: hello@collectoradvantage.com.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name as Google provides it. We do not receive your Google password.
  • What you enter. Books you own and want, grades, prices you paid, budgets, alert preferences and similar settings.
  • Listing details from the extension. When you open a marketplace listing or a page of sold results with the extension installed, it reads the listing details shown on that page (title, price, shipping, item number, photo and sale date) and sends them to your account. It does not read other websites, your messages, your payment details or your browsing history.
  • Connection keys. A key that connects the extension to your account. We store only an irreversible hash of it, and you can disconnect the extension at any time.
  • Technical data. A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in, and standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) used to keep the service running and secure.

We do not use advertising cookies or sell personal information.

How we use it

  • To run the service: show your collection and hunt, assess listings against your budget, and send alerts you ask for.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To maintain and improve the service, including fixing errors in how listings are read.

Who we share it with

Only the providers we need to run the service, under their own terms: an email delivery service for sign-in codes and alerts, Google if you choose Google sign-in, a hosting provider once the service is online, and optional notification services you turn on (such as a phone notification topic). We may also share information if the law requires it. We do not share your collection, hunt or budgets with other users.

The service shows information from marketplaces and other third-party sources. That is information about listings and sales, not about you.

Marketplace names

Collector Advantage is independent. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by eBay or any grading company. eBay is a trademark of eBay Inc.

Affiliate links

Links to marketplaces may be affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. When we show such links in the product we say so beside them.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. You can delete your account from the account page, which removes your collection, hunt, alerts, settings and connection keys. Backups, if any, are overwritten on a regular schedule. Sale records that came from marketplaces, and are not about you, may be kept after you leave.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and data from the account page, or email us and we will do it.
  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Remove the extension, or disconnect it from your account page.
  • Turn off alerts in your settings.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your data, including signing in with one-time codes or Google rather than passwords. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute protection.

Children

Collector Advantage is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell account holders by email.
