Built around how you actually collect

Every tool feeds the next decision, so you spend less time searching and second-guessing, and more time buying the right copies.

  1. Set your hunt

    The books you want, the grades that would do, and what you're willing to pay.

  2. Check any copy

    Confirm it's the exact book and grade, and see what matching copies really sold for.

  3. Buy and log it

    Your purchase and what it actually cost you go straight into your collection.

  4. Upgrade smarter

    Every listing is weighed against what you already own and the budget you set.

One place for your collecting

Three spaces that work together, so every buying decision has the full picture behind it.

My Collection

What you own, what you paid all-in, what it's worth now, and what has changed.

My Hunt

The books you want, the versions and grades that would do, your budget, and the upgrades you're after.

My Opportunities

Copies that match what you're hunting for, with the reason each one is worth a look.
